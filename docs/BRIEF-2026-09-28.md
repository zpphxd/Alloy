## Economics

These are Paul's rules of thumb. They're implemented in `src/economics/estimate.ts` and pinned by tests.

| Example | Math |
|---|---|
| 200 MW operating solar (TX) | ~$2.4M premium → ~$240k commission. "We're one quarter of the way there with one operating solar project in Texas." |
| 600 MW solar, construction | ~$3M builder's risk premium. Tax limit = 40% of ~$600M value, grossed up for taxes ≈ $300M. Tax commission = 1% of limit ≈ $3M. Producer: 15% of the tax commission (~$450k) + 45% of the P&C commission (~$135k) ≈ **$585k**. |
| 100 MW storage | ~$1M premium (~$100k commission) + ~$50M tax policy (~$500k) ≈ $1.5M |
| RNG project | $10-30M project; premium "a couple hundred grand" |
| Bitdeer Canada (live deal) | ~$300M; ~$1.2M builder's risk. No tax policy (crypto mining + power plant). |

Tax insurance applies mostly to solar, storage, wind, and RNG, not to crypto mining or gas plants. It's where the money is: "Sarah... has twelve million in production this year. And eleven million of it's tax." Tax and reps & warranties policies are one-time, but the right client gives "multiple bites of the apple."

**Open question for Paul.** At one point the 600 MW example is described as "six million in commission" ($3M premium + $3M tax commission), but the rest of the math treats P&C at 10% commission. The model uses 10% P&C and 1%-of-limit tax. Confirm which one is right.
